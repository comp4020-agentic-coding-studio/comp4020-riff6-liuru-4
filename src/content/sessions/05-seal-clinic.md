## Before the seminar

Bring your attribution-report draft, even unfinished. This is the last
seminar before it's due.

## In the seminar

Idris runs the seal-matching exercise from lecture against a reference set
of dated impressions, including at least one known forged or recut seal
students have to catch without being told which one it is. The last half
hour is a swap: exchange drafts with a partner and mark, on paper, the one
claim in their note you'd push hardest on.

## Afterwards

The attribution note is due Friday, midday. It should already reflect
whatever your partner pushed on --- a note that ignores a specific,
named objection raised in seminar reads as unfinished, not as confident.
